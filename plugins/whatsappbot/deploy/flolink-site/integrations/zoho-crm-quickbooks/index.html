Zoho CRM + QuickBooks Online integration

Keep Zoho CRM and QuickBooks Online in step. Vendors, customers and invoices you manage in CRM are created or updated in QuickBooks, with the QuickBooks IDs saved back to CRM.

Book a free 20-minute call See how it works

The problem

  • Accounts team rekeys CRM records into QuickBooks
  • Vendor and customer details differ between systems
  • No way to see QuickBooks status from inside CRM
  • Duplicate records when the same name is typed twice

What we build

  • Vendors and customers created or updated in QuickBooks from CRM
  • Invoices pushed to QuickBooks when a deal closes
  • QuickBooks IDs stored in CRM to prevent duplicates
  • Sync status and errors visible on the CRM record
  • Secure OAuth connection to QuickBooks Online
How it works

From QuickBooks Online to Zoho in four steps

1

Record saved

A vendor, customer or invoice is saved in Zoho CRM.

2

Match check

Deluge looks up the record in QuickBooks by ID or display name.

3

Create or update

The record is created or updated through the QuickBooks API.

4

ID saved back

The QuickBooks ID and sync status are written back to CRM.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it work with QuickBooks Desktop?

This integration is for QuickBooks Online. Talk to us if you use QuickBooks Desktop.

Can it sync both ways?

Yes. Most teams start with CRM to QuickBooks, and we can add payment or status updates flowing back to CRM.

What about existing records?

We run a one-time match so records that already exist in both systems are linked, not duplicated.
