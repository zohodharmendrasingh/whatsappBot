The problem

  • Orders copied from Shopify into Zoho by hand
  • Wrong SKUs, prices or tax when orders are rekeyed

What we build

  • New Shopify orders create sales orders or invoices in Zoho Books or CRM

  • Your existing Zoho workflows and approvals still run
  • Multi-store and stock sync options when you need them
How it works

From Shopify to Zoho in four steps

1

Order placed

Shopify sends the new order to Zoho Flow or a webhook.

2

Order read

A Deluge function reads the customer, address and line items.

3

Items matched

Each SKU is matched to the item in Zoho Books or the product in CRM.

4

Record created

The sales order or invoice is created and your workflows run.

FAQ

Common questions

Can orders go into Zoho CRM instead of Zoho Books?

Yes. We can create sales orders in Zoho CRM, sales orders or invoices in Zoho Books, or both, depending on how your team works.

What happens if a SKU is not found in Zoho?

We agree the rule with you up front: skip and alert, create the item, or hold the order for review. Every run is logged so nothing is lost.

Do I need Zoho Flow?

Not always. Simple setups run on Zoho Flow; higher volumes or multi-store setups can run on webhooks and Deluge or Zoho Catalyst.
