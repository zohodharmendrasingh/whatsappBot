Zoho Books + WooCommerce integration

WooCommerce orders flow into Zoho Books as sales orders or invoices, customers are created or matched by email, and contacts reach Zoho CRM, all without copy and paste.

The problem

  • Downloading WooCommerce orders and re-entering them in Zoho Books
  • Customers typed twice, with different spellings
  • Payments recorded late, so reports are always behind
  • Sales team can’t see web customers in Zoho CRM

What we build

  • Orders create sales orders or invoices in Zoho Books
  • Customers created or updated by email, no duplicates
  • Payments recorded against the invoice
  • Customers and orders visible in Zoho CRM
  • Batch import of past customers and orders
How it works

From WooCommerce to Zoho in four steps

1

Order placed

WooCommerce sends the order by webhook or Zoho Flow.

2

Customer matched

The customer is found by email, or created in Books and CRM.

3

Items mapped

Items, quantities, tax and shipping are mapped to Zoho Books.

4

Invoice & payment

The sales order or invoice is created and the payment recorded.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you bring in my existing WooCommerce customers?

Yes. We run a one-time batch import that creates or updates customers in Zoho CRM and Zoho Books, then keep new ones in sync.

Does it handle tax and shipping?

Yes. Tax and shipping lines are mapped to your Zoho Books tax rates and shipping charge, so totals match WooCommerce.

Will it work with my WooCommerce plugins?

Usually. We check your order data during the free call; custom fields from plugins can be mapped to Zoho custom fields.
