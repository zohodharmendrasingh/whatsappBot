How it works

From WordPress to Zoho in four steps

1

Form submitted

The WordPress form or booking sends its data by webhook.

2

Contact created

A Deluge function creates or updates the customer and CRM contact.

3

Invoice created

An invoice is created in Zoho Books for the service or product.

4

Payment recorded

Online payments are recorded so the invoice shows as paid.

FAQ

Common questions

Which WordPress form plugins work?

Any plugin that can send a webhook or connect to Zoho Flow. We confirm yours during the free call.

Can it create leads instead of customers?

Yes. Enquiry forms can create leads in Zoho CRM, while paid bookings create customers and invoices in Zoho Books.

Is my website slowed down?

No. The website only sends one small request; the Zoho work happens in Zoho Flow or Deluge.
