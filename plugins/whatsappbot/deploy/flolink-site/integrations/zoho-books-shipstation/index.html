Zoho Books + ShipStation integration

Confirmed sales orders in Zoho Books are sent to ShipStation automatically, and shipment status and tracking numbers come back into Zoho, so sales and support always know where an order is.

Book a free 20-minute call See how it works

The problem

  • Orders copied from Zoho Books into ShipStation by hand
  • Tracking numbers emailed around instead of stored
  • Customers asking “where’s my order?” and nobody knows
  • Shipped orders still open in Zoho Books

What we build

  • Sales orders created in ShipStation from Zoho Books
  • Items, weights and ship-to address mapped
  • Shipment status and tracking written back to Zoho
  • Orders marked shipped in Zoho Books
  • Runs on Zoho Flow with a secure ShipStation connection
How it works

From ShipStation to Zoho in four steps

1

Order confirmed

A sales order is confirmed in Zoho Books.

2

Sent to ShipStation

A Zoho Flow function creates the order in ShipStation.

3

Label printed

Your team prints the label in ShipStation as usual.

4

Tracking back

Status and tracking number are written back to Zoho Books.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we keep printing labels in ShipStation?

Yes. Your warehouse keeps using ShipStation; the integration only moves the order in and the tracking out.

Can customers get tracking automatically?

Yes. Once tracking is in Zoho Books it can be emailed to the customer or shown in Zoho CRM.

Does it work with Zoho Inventory too?

Yes. The same approach works with Zoho Inventory sales orders and packages.
